The new builder and strict checker now share the complete canonical ordered inventory independently of the submitted manifest. Re-signed omissions, out-of-inventory sources and reordered bundles fail. Archive checking retains its embedded-source responsibility independently of later workspace sources. Eleven publication regressions cover these boundaries, canonical text/binary bytes, version separation and malformed inventories. The reviewer inspects the actual forged PDF as well as the unit regressions.

The primary reviewer covered pages 73–128, including complete model sources, release notes, helper-access examples, worked figures, support tables and both new decisions. Normal chapter-ending whitespace and wrapped source continuations preserve the original content.
